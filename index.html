  • Appeals Court Rejects DOJ Bid to Set Aside ~20,000 California Mail Ballots: The Justice Department asked the 9th Circuit to order California to segregate up to 20,000 ballots cast by voters on a federal list of suspected noncitizens, and to investigate their citizenship mid-election. DOJ sent the list to Los Angeles County Oct. 13 and sued Oct. 15; state officials called the list deeply flawed and outdated. After emergency arguments Oct. 25, a three-judge panel ruled 2-1 on Oct. 26 that it lacked jurisdiction and sent the case back to the district court, which holds its first hearing Oct. 27. Relevant to CA-13, CA-22 and CA-48 ballot counting. Election Day is Nov. 3. Read the Votebeat report
